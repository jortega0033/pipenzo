Foundations

Tokens and primitives, fixed before any product screen. Stepped near-black surfaces do the layering instead of border lines; every control is a pill or a circle; type is either bold or regular. The single accent is GitHub's own green, so a status colour means the same thing here as it does on github.com. Component anatomy follows GitHub Primer where Primer has an opinion (banner, flash, toast, form control) and IBM Carbon where it doesn't (tile, carousel).

Risk-graded approval — LOW proceeds and is logged, MEDIUM blocks on a compact inline card, HIGH gets the full card plus an OS notification

About 95% of actions are LOW: routine edits inside the owned worktree, reads, greps. They get one muted line in the stream and nothing else. MEDIUM and HIGH both carry the pre-commitment record and a reject-reason field; only MEDIUM has Undo, only HIGH pings the OS immediately. Publishing is permanently HIGH — no auto-allow exists for it at any level. A cumulative strip per ticket fills as unreviewed LOW actions and mispredictions pile up, and promotes the next MEDIUM to HIGH once it crosses the line.
